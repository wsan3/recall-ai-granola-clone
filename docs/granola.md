Granola is an AI-powered meeting notepad and note-taking app that turns your own lightweight notes plus the meeting audio into structured, comprehensive documentation so you can stay present instead of furiously typing. [gptbot](https://gptbot.io/ai-tools/granola)

## What Granola is

Granola runs alongside your meetings and lets you jot quick bullets or phrases while it automatically builds out full, organized notes behind the scenes.  It’s designed for people who spend a lot of time in back-to-back calls (PMs, founders, sales, research, agencies) and want meeting notes that feel like they were written by a human, not a raw transcript bot. [mrktcorrect](https://mrktcorrect.com/blog/granola-ai-review)

## Core product idea

- You take sparse, high-level notes during the call.
- Granola records and/or listens to the meeting, aligns your notes to what was actually said, and expands them.
- After the meeting, you get a structured doc with sections, bullets, and key quotes, plus tools to create follow-ups, summaries, and action items. [zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/granola-ai/)

This approach tries to be less intrusive than “join-as-a-bot” recorders while giving you higher quality notes than just transcription. [bluedothq](https://www.bluedothq.com/blog/granola-review)

## Core features

These are the main features you should know about:

- AI-augmented notepad  
  You type short bullets; Granola follows your lead and turns them into fleshed-out notes with headings, sub-bullets, and clearer wording.  It keeps a clear visual distinction between what you wrote (e.g., black text) and what the AI added (e.g., gray text) so you always know which is which. [gptbot](https://gptbot.io/ai-tools/granola)

- Automatic structure and hierarchy  
  The app organizes content into sections (agenda, discussion points, decisions, action items, next steps) instead of giving you one long wall of text.  This makes it easier to skim and share notes with teammates after the call. [mrktcorrect](https://mrktcorrect.com/blog/granola-ai-review)

- Quote “zoom in” and source traceability  
  You can click into a note or summary line and “zoom in” to see the exact quote or part of the conversation it came from.  That helps you verify accuracy and avoid misattributing decisions or commitments. [zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/granola-ai/)

- Lightweight, bot-free workflow  
  Unlike some tools that add a visible bot attendee to your Zoom/Meet/Teams call, Granola emphasizes working in the background without intrusive bots or complex integrations.  That can reduce friction with guests or clients who are wary of recorded meetings. [bluedothq](https://www.bluedothq.com/blog/granola-review)

- Post-meeting actions  
  From the notes, you can generate follow-up emails, summaries, or reports and extract action items.  This is aimed at making it easier to push outcomes into your existing workflow, not just archive the meeting. [gptbot](https://gptbot.io/ai-tools/granola)

- Meeting-focused UX  
  The interface is tuned for live meetings: minimal clutter, fast note entry, and AI filling in context.  It is particularly pitched for user research sessions, internal standups, client calls, and strategy meetings where attention and rapport matter. [mrktcorrect](https://mrktcorrect.com/blog/granola-ai-review)

## Typical use cases

Common scenarios where Granola tends to fit:

- Standups and internal syncs: auto-capture decisions, blockers, and owners so you don’t have to manually write summaries after every recurring meeting. [mrktcorrect](https://mrktcorrect.com/blog/granola-ai-review)
